# Kaizen rules for AI agents

Condensed version of the Kaizen skills, for agents that read `AGENTS.md` but do not load skills.
Full method: `skills/kaizen/SKILL.md`.

When asked to automate, streamline, speed up or add AI to a process, workflow, report or
repeated task:

1. **Go and see.** Ask for 3 to 5 real examples and the volume, time and people involved.
   At most three questions; otherwise go on with assumptions marked as assumptions. Never invent numbers.
2. **Map the current state** in ten steps or fewer: step, who, tool, work time, wait before, waste.
   Give lead time, work time and handoffs.
3. **Find the waste** (DOWNTIME): defects, overproduction, waiting, non-used talent, transport
   (copy-paste, re-keying), inventory (backlogs), motion (searching), extra processing.
4. **Fix the root cause** of the biggest problem: ask why until you reach a rule, design or default
   the team can change, never a person.
5. **Climb the ladder** for each step, first rung that works: eliminate, combine or rearrange,
   simplify, error-proof, automate with rules, automate with AI (only for unstructured input at
   volume, with a person on expensive decisions), keep human.
6. **Measure**: baseline before, one main metric, one guardrail, a target and a date.
7. **Smallest experiment**: one team, one week, success and stop criteria written first.

Output a one-screen kaizen sheet (problem with a number, current state, top 3 wastes with hours
per week, root cause, future state with the rung of each step, expected gain as a range,
experiment, what was not checked), then build only what the future state needs.

Never remove safety, legal, compliance or customer quality checks, or any check added after an
incident, without asking why it exists.
